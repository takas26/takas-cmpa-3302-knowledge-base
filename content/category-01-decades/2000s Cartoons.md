# List of Cartoons from the 2000s (2000-2009)

## Broadcast Networks
As mentioned in Networks - [[Broadcast Networks]], these cartoons where aired through local affiliate channels. The 2000s were the end for most regular broadcast cartoons as new animated channels were created to compete with local broadcasts. Fox was the only channel that was broadcasting cartoons during primetime on Sunday nights. With the creation of Animation Domination in 2005, this block was aimed at adults with adult themes and humor, and has been the home of The Simpsons for almost 40 years.

## Cartoon Network
As mentioned in Networks - [[category-02-networks/Cartoon Network|Cartoon Network]], the early 2000s saw the channel expand its original programming with unforgettable and beloved shows. This new wave of content increased Cartoon Network's household reach and transformed it into an industry powerhouse. This also includes the introduction of **Adult Swim**, its block of adult theme cartoons.

## Disney Channel
As mentioned in Networks - [[Disney Channel]], the channel stopped broadcasting its cartoons through local broadcast channels and migrated all of them to the Disney Channel, including new content. Although their animation studio was incredibly successful, it was not their main priority since their focus was also on live-action original series.
