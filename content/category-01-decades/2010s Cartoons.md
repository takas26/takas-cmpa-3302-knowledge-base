# List of Cartoons from the 2010s (2010-2019)

## Broadcast Networks
As mentioned in Networks - [[Broadcast Networks]], by the 2010s, all local cartoon broadcasting was moved to premier cartoon channels or cable programming. There were very few original cartoon broadcasts specific to local channels. This is when we start seeing a shift from local channels and cable programming to streaming apps. Fox was still the only channel broadcasting animation shows during primetime Sundays with their adult themed cartoons.
