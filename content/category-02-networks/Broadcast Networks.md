# Fox Kids
1990-2002   

Famous quote from the Bobby's World cartoon:
> Bobby, I love you!

## History
Officially launched in September 1990, Fox Kids revolutionized Saturday morning cartoons for over a decade due to its fun, action-packed, and edgier lineup that no other competitor was offering at that time. It revolutionized the superhero animation genre with cartoons like Batman: The Animated Series, X-Men: The Animated Series, and Spider-Man: The Animated Series. Although toy lines were still the main focus and reason for cartoons at that time, the network also prioritized on great story telling, ground-breaking animation, and proved that children's programming can be appealing to adults. Several series even being nominated and winning several Emmy Daytime awards.
Once WB Kids was created to rival Fox Kids, the network started to lose traction due to some of its lineup being moved to WB Kids, and cartoons like Pokémon, which was a worldwide sensation. Eventually Fox sold off its family entertainment division to Walt Disney in 2001. In late 2002, Fox Kids was rebranded to 4Kids TV and most shows were moved to ABC Kids, which was the final nail in the coffin for the network.

# Kids' WB
1995-2008

Famous quote by Brain from Pinky and the Brain:
> Are you pondering what I'm pondering?

## History
Kids' WB was launched in September of 1995 to compete with the extremely successful programing at Fox Kids.  Because WB animation studio produced most of the content for Fox Kids, when the network was launched, it brought several cartoons with it (Animaniacs, Batman). The network was also created to help its parent network, the WB channel, to bring an existing, huge, and dedicated children's audience to its market.
What made the network extremely successful and be dominant in the children's cartoon market was the acquisition the English dub right to Pokémon. Its broadcast was a massive ratings hit, which buried its competitor Fox Kids. Its success brought other action-oriented anime shows like Yu-Gi-Oh!, and Dragon Ball Z.
The end of Kid's WB started when Time Warner and CBS announced the merger of the WB and UPN to form The CW. Kids' WB was moved in September of 2006 to The CW, losing the Pokémon franchise to cable networks, thus only airing it remaining lineup for an additional two years before it was shut down in 2008.
