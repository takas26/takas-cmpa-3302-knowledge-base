# Cartoon Network
1992-Present

Famous quote by the The Powerpuff Girls show:
> Sugar, spice, and everything nice. These are the ingredients chosen to create the perfect little girls.

## History
Cartoon Network launched in October 1st, 1992. It was the first 24-hour cable channel network completely dedicated to animation. It acquired famous libraries from MGM and Hanna-Barbera and was mostly airing reruns of classic cartoons during its first two years like Looney Tunes, Tom & Jerry, The Flintstones and Scooby-Doo. 
The network's breakthrough into original programming came in 1995 when the channel gave animators creative freedom, with high budgets to create short pilots for new and innovative cartoons. Known as the "Cartoon Cartoons" era, the network produced huge hits like Dexter's Laboratory, Johnny Bravo, The Powerpuff Girls, and Courage the Cowardly Dog. 
Due to its continued success in original programming, Cartoon Network expanded its lineup and took new chances with its animation, themes, tone, and style to give us some amazing cartoons from 1996 all the way to today.
### Adult Swim
2001-Present
Another reason why Cartoon Network was so groundbreaking and successful was due to its implementation of Adult Swim in 2001, which was late-night cartoons targeting older teens and adults. As kids grew up watching the channel in the mid-90s to early 2000s, Cartoon Network knew that it would lose its target audience as they grew older. Adult Swim was created to host shows like Rick and Morty and The Boondocks.
### Toonami
1997-2008 & 2012-Present
The toonami block was famous for the American introduction of anime shows like Dragon Ball Z, Sailor Moon and Gundam Wing. It later introduced American audiences to anime like Naruto, and One Piece. Although the block was cancelled in 2008, it was revived as a new block within Adult Swim where it currently resides airing modern anime like My Hero Academia, and Attack on Titan.
