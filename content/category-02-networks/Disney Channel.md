# Disney Channel
1983-Present

Famous quote by Phineas from Phineas and Ferb:
> I know what we're going to do today!

## History
The Disney Channel was officially launched in April of 1983 broadcasting reruns of classic theatrical shorts of its beloved characters Mickey, Minnie, Donald and Goofy. It was created as a premium subscription service that transitioned to basic cable in 1993. The network didn't have the typical children's cartoon block until 1990 when it created the syndication block of The Disney Afternoon and the ABC Weekend Morning lineup. These were broadcasted locally through station affiliates like Fox and ABC depending on the region. Disney Afternoon and ABC Weekend Morning rivaled other networks like Fox Kids and Kids' WB. Competition for children's attention was heavy during the weekdays after school and Saturday mornings. 
In 1997, The Disney Channel shifted its focus to children and teens and began producing its own original cartoons specifically for the Disney Channel. Since the channel was now part of basic cable, its viewership skyrocketed with hit shows like Kimpossible, Lilo & Stich: The Series  and The Proud Family. Additional shows like Phineas and Ferb, considered to be the most successful and longest running Disney cartoon, cemented the Disney Channel's future for serialized, mystery-driven storytelling that appealed to older audiences and fans of overarching plots and lore. Shows like Gravity Falls paved the way to even more culturally significant series like Star vs. The Forces of Evil, The Owl House, and Amphibia. It is this commitment to quality and unique storytelling that has pushed Disney to create new and exciting shows for new audiences, with no end in sight.
