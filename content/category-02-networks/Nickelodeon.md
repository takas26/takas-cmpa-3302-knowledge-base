# Nickelodeon (Nicktoons)
1990-Present

Famous quote by Spongebob Squarepants:
> I'm ready! I'm ready! I'm ready!

## History
Nickelodeon, also known as "Nicktoons", debuted in 1991 by launching its own line of original animated series with the premier of three hall of fame cartoons: Rugrats, Doug, and The Ren & Stimpy Show. Although it created some great cartoons with great animation and storylines like Rocko's Modern Life and Hey Arnold!, it wasn't until it created its most recognizable character of all time, that it propelled them in to the powerhouse it is known as today: Spongebob Squarepants! Premiering in 1999, Nickelodeon expanded its cartoon roster due to the huge success of Spongebob. By 2004. it had created seven additional animation hits like The Fairly Odd Parents, Jimmy Neutron, and Invader Zim. All that hard work would pay off when their following hit would air in 2005: Avatar: The Last Airbender! The anime style cartoon would be a huge success for Nickelodeon at that time since anime was being introduced to the American audience by networks like Cartoon Network. Nickelodeon capitalized on that success and renewed Avatar for an additional two seasons due to its prime demographic of 6 year old's to young adults.
Because of its long success and continued breakthroughs in animation, Nickelodeon continues to air all of its cartoons due to its lovable characters and unique stories.
