# PBS Kids
1994-Present

Famous quote by Mrs. Frizzle from The Magic School Bus:
> Take chances, make mistakes, get messy.

## History
Although PBS has been dedicated to broadcasting children's foundational educational shows since 1971, it did not introduce animated cartoons into its programing until 1994. Known as the PTV era, it introduced children to shows like The Magic School Bus, Arthur and Dragon Tales. Shows that introduced kids to science, interpersonal relationships, daily life, and using your imagination. As cartoon block became more popular, PBS started adding even more animation programing to appeal to middle-childhood learning with shows like Maya & Miguel, WordGirl, and  its most popular series Curious George.
PBS Kids has continued to air fantastic children's programing for free for over 22 years. The network deserves a spot on this list since it has provided several generations with age appropriate, whole-child development by teaching children how to succeed in school, spark curiosity and how to foster crucial life skills.
