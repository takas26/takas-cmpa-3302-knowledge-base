# Genres
Below you will find a comprehensive genre breakdown for each network. We will focus on their main genre, and provide a quick overview for each network.

### Fox Kids
- Genres: Action, Comedy, Sci-Fi
- Overview: Was focused on high-intensity animated action and comic book properties (Spiderman, X-Men), paired with slapstick cartoons (Animaniacs, Freakazoid!)
- Genre Breakdown:
	- Action & Sci-Fi (60%)
	- Comedy (40%)
### Kids' WB
- Genres: Action, Comedy, Fantasy, and Sci-Fi
- Overview: Popularized Japanese anime imports (Pokemon, Yu-Gi-Oh!) in the U.S. while focusing on properties like DC comics (Superman, Justice League, Static Shock) and classic WB slapstick comedy.
- Genre Breakdown:
	- Fantasy (40%)
	- Action & Sci-Fi (30%)
	- Comedy (30%)
### Cartoon Network
- Genres: Action, Comedy, Fantasy, and Sci-Fi
- Overview: A powerhouse centered on artist created properties, lore-heavy adventure animation and story-rich animated fantasy and sci-fi worlds.
- Genre Breakdown:
	- Comedy (45%)
	- Fantasy (30%)
	- Action & Sci-Fi (25%)
### Adult Swim
- Genres: Action, Comedy, Fantasy, Sci-Fi
- Overview: Cartoon Network's late-night block targeting adults with experimental animated comedy and over the top action.
- Genre Breakdown:
	- Comedy (60%)
	- Action & Sci-Fi (30%)
	- Fantasy (10%)
### Disney Channel
- Genres: Action, Comedy, Drama, Fantasy, Sci-Fi
- Overview: Disney's animation department produced massive hits mixing comedy with deep, serialized fantasy, drama, and action.
- Genre Breakdown:
	- Comedy & Sci-Fi (40%)
	- Fantasy & Drama (40%)
	- Action (20%)
### Nickelodeon
- Genres: Action, Comedy, Fantasy, Sci-Fi, Educational
- Overview: Built an animation empire with Nicktoons, world-building fantasy sagas, and early childhood animated learning.
- Genre Breakdown:
	- Comedy (45%)
	- Fantasy & Action (25%)
	- Sci-Fi (15%)
	- Educational (15%)
### PBS Kids
- Genres: Educational, Comedy, Sci-Fi
- Overview: A public service dedicated entirely to non-commercial, research-backed animated educational programming.
- Genre Breakdown:
	- Educational & Comedy (60%)
	  Educational & Sci-Fi (40%)
