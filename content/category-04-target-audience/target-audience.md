## TV Parental Guidelines
First introduced in 1997 due to public concerns of TV programing broadcasting an increased amount of mature content.  It consists of six age-based ratings with content descriptors which specified each content type.

#### Children's Ratings (Ages 0-11)
- TV-Y: Appropriate for all children. Specific for ages 2-6
- TV-Y7: Directed to older children between the ages of 7-11
- TV-Y7-FV: Directed to children age 7 and older, featuring more intense or combative Fantasy Violence
#### General and Mature Ratings (Ages 7+)
- TV-G: Suitable for a general audience
- TV-PG: Parental Guidance suggested. May contain material unsuitable for younger children
- TV-14: Parents strongly cautioned. May be unsuitable for children under 14
- TV-MA: Mature Audience only. Designated for adults and may be unsuitable for anyone under the age of 17
#### Content Descriptors
- D: Suggestive Language
- L: Adult language
- S: Sexual situations
- V: Violence
- FV: Fantasy Violence (Only used with TV-Y7)
