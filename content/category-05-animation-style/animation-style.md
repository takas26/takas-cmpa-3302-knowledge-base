# Animation Style
Below is a comprehensive breakdown of the animation styles used by each network, as well as the overall look and feel each network was trying to achieve.

### Fox Kids
- Primary Animation Styles: Traditional 2D Cell animation, Japanese anime, and early 3D CGI.
- Look & Feel: High-Contrast, dynamic action, dark outlines, and superhero comic art.

### Kids' WB
- Primary Animation Styles: Traditional 2D Cell animation, Japanese anime, Digital Vector.
- Look & Feel: Polished, smooth, sleek, and high-energy with dramatic visual pacing.

### Cartoon Network
- Primary Animation Styles: Stylized geometric 2D animation, Digital Vector.
- Look & Feel: Creator-driven, graphic, visually bold and colorful.

### Disney Channel
- Primary Animation Style: Traditional 2D Cell animation, clean Digital Vector 2D, Storyboard-driven Hand-drawn animation.
- Look & Feel: Bright, expressive, high production value, warm colors, and cinematic landscapes.

### Nickelodeon
- Primary Animation Style: Hand-drawn 2D (Also known as "Nicktoon" style), Full 3D CGI.
- Look & Feel: Exaggerated, tactile, vibrant, kid-centric.

### PBS Kids
- Primary Animation Style: Storybook Illustrative 2D, Digital Vector.
- Look & Feel: Soft, inviting, high-readability, safe.
