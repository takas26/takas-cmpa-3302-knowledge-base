# Accolades
Below is a list of all the animated cartoon shows that were nominated and/or won an award by Network, Decade, Title, Award(s) and Year. This is not a complete list to all the awards and accolades each network and TV show was awarded or nominated for.

### Broadcast Network
### Decades
##### 1980s
- The Simpsons - 37 Primetime Emmy Wins (1990-2026), 34 Annie Awards (1992-2002), 2 Peabody Awards (1996, 2019), Hollywood Walk of Fame Star (200) 
##### 1990s
- Batman: The Animated Series - 4 Daytime Emmy Wins (2x1993, 1994, 1995)
- Animaniacs - 8 Daytime Emmy Wins (1995, 1996, 1997, 1998, 1999), 1 Peabody Award (1993)
- King of the Hill - 2 Primetime Emmy Wins (1999, 2002), 5 Primetime Emmy Nominations (1997, 1998, 2001, 2007, 2008)
- Family Guy - 9 Primetime Emmy Wins (2000, 2002, 2007, 2010, 2016, 2017, 2018)
##### 2000s
- X-Men: Evolution - 2 Daytime Emmy Wins (2001, 2003)
- The Batman - 6 Daytime Emmy Wins (2005, 2006, 2007, 2008)
##### 2010s
- Bob's Burgers - 2 Primetime Emmy Wins (2014, 2017) Kidscreen Best Animated Series Award (2026)
##### 2020s
- The Great North - 1 GLAAD Media Award Nomination (2022)
### Cartoon Network
#### Decades
##### 1980s
- None
##### 1990s
- Dexter's Laboratory - 4 Emmy Nominations (1996, 1997, 1998, 2002)
- The Powerpuff Girls - 2 Primetime Emmy Wins (2000, 2005), 3 Primetime Emmy Nominations (1999, 2001, 2004)
- Courage the Cowardly Dog - 1 Academy Award Nomination (1996)
##### 2000s
- Samurai Jack - 8 Primetime Emmy Wins (2x2003, 2004, 2005, 4x2017)
- The Grim Adventures of Billy & Mandy - 1 Emmy Win (2006), 1 Annie Award Win (2005)
- Ben 10 - 1 Emmy Win (2007)
##### 2010s
- Adventure Time - 8 Primetime Emmy Wins (2013, 2014, 2015, 2016, 2017, 2018), 3 Annie Awards (2014, 2x2017), 1 Peabody Award (2015)
- Regular Show - 1 Primetime Emmy Win (2012), 5 Primetime Emmy Nominations (2011, 2013, 2014, 2015)
- Steven Universe - 1 Peabody Award (2019), 5 Emmy Nominations (2015, 2016, 2017, 2018, 2019)
##### 2020s
- Craig of the Creek - 2 GLAAD Media Award Nominations (2021, 2023, 2024), 4 Daytime Emmy Nominations (2020, 2021, 2023, 2025)

### Disney Channel
#### Decades
##### 1980s
- DuckTales - 2 Emmy Nominations (1988, 1989)
##### 1990s
- Gargoyles - 1 Emmy Nomination (1995)
- Recess - 1 Emmy Nomination (1998)
##### 2000s
- Kim Possible - 1 Daytime Emmy Win (2005), 4 Daytime Emmy Nominations (2004, 2005, 2006)
- Phineas and Ferb - 3 Primetime Emmy Wins (2010, 2011, 2012), 1 Daytime Emmy Nomination (2009)
##### 2010s
- Gravity Falls - 2 Primetime Emmy Wins (2014, 2015), 3 Annie Wins (2013, 2015, 2016)
- Star vs The Forces of Evil - 2 Emmy Wins (2018, 2019)
##### 2020s
- Owl House - 1 Peabody Award (2021)
- Amphibia - 1 Emmy Nomination (2022), 1 Annie Win (2021)
### Nickelodeon
#### Decades
##### 1980s
- None
##### 1990s
- Rugrats - 4 Daytime Emmy Wins (1993, 2001, 2003, 2004), Hollywood Walk of Fame Star (2001)
- The Ren & Stimpy Show - 3 Emmy Nominations (1992, 1993, 1994)
- SpongeBob SquarePants - 4 Daytime Emmy Wins (2010, 2014, 2018, 2020), 6 Annie Awards (2005, 2006, 2010, 2011, 2013, 2019)
##### 2000s
- Avatar: The Last Airbender - 1 Peabody Award (2008), 1 Emmy Win (2007), 5 Annie Wins (2006, 2x2007, 2x2009)
- The Fairly OddParents - 2 Daytime Emmy Wins (2010, 2011), 1 Primetime Emmy Nomination (2002)
- Invader Zim - 1 Primetime Emmy Win (2001), 1 Annie Win (2001)
##### 2010s
- The Legend of Korra - 4 Daytime Emmy Wins (2013, 2014, 2015)
- The Loud House - 2 Daytime Emmy Wins (2018, 2019)
##### 2020s
The Patrick Star Show - 1 Annie Award Nomination (2022)
### PBS Kids
#### Decades
##### 1980s
- None
##### 1990s
- Arthur - 4 Daytime Emmy Wins (1998, 1999, 2001, 2007), 1 Peabody Award (1997)
- The Magic School Bus - 1 Daytime Emmy Win (1995), 4 Daytime Emmy Nominations (1995, 1996, 1997, 1998)
##### 2000s
 - Curious George - 2 Daytime Emmy Wins (2008, 2010)
 - WordGirl - 4 Daytime Emmy Wins (2008, 2012, 2013, 2015)
